# DECINT — "empty shield" identity

## The idea

One hue, all the way through. The mark is an **empty** violet shield on
near-black, and every accent in the interface is that same violet — so nothing
on the page introduces a colour the logo doesn't already contain.

The previous violet→cyan gradient and its midpoint blue (`#4989E5`) are retired.
Two hues reading as one brand asked the eye to do work the mark never justified.

## Palette

| Token | Was | Now | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| `--color-bg` | `#0F1622` | `#0B0C13` | one step below the plate |
| `--color-surface` | `#1A2435` | `#10121C` | sampled from your screenshot (89% of its pixels) |
| `--color-text` | `#E9EDF5` | `#E9E9ED` | sampled from your screenshot |
| `--color-accent` | `#4989E5` blue | `#8D5BF6` violet | your existing brand violet, unchanged |
| `--grad-from` / `--grad-to` | `#8D5BF6` → `#04B6D3` | `#B594FF` → `#7C4DEE` | single-hue ramp, no cyan |

Neutrals are now violet-biased rather than blue-biased, so greys sit beside the
accent instead of arguing with it.

## The mark

- The shield is **hollow at every size**, including 16px. Nothing goes inside it.
- Stroke weight down from `0.05` to `0.042` of the icon size — the "sleek" part.
- Plate is flat `#10121C`. The old gradient plate glowed in a dark tab strip.
- `mark-512.png` (the plate-less version) now has a genuinely **transparent**
  interior. It was rendering solid, because once the stroke and the fill were
  both violet the hollow punch had nothing to punch against.
- Geometry is unchanged — same silhouette, generated by the same function, so
  the icon and the wordmark still match exactly.

## Wordmark

Weight 600 → 500, tracking `0.16em` → `0.26em`. The letters read as a set of
marks rather than a word, which is what makes it feel engineered instead of
typed. Now defined once in `components/site/Wordmark.tsx` and shared by the nav
and the footer — previously each declared its own spacing and they had already
drifted apart by 2px and one weight step.
